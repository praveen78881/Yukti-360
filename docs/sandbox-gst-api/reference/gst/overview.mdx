Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a comprehensive indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India. Implemented on July 1st, 2017, GST replaced multiple indirect taxes and unified the Indian tax system. As a consumption-based tax, GST is applied at each point of sale in the supply chain.

## GST Registration Requirements

Businesses must register under GST if they meet the following criteria:

| **Supply Type** | **Annual Turnover Threshold** |
| :-: | :-: |
| Goods | ₹40 Lakhs |
| Services | ₹20 Lakhs |

<Info>
  Businesses operating in special category states have lower thresholds: ₹20 Lakhs for goods and ₹10 Lakhs for services.
</Info>

## The GST Compliance Challenge

Managing GST compliance is complex and time-consuming. Businesses must:

- **Generate GST-compliant invoices** with proper tax calculations
- **File monthly and annual returns** (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9)
- **Reconcile purchase and sales ledgers** with supplier data
- **Claim Input Tax Credit (ITC)** accurately
- **Generate E-Invoices** for B2B transactions (mandatory for businesses with turnover \> ₹5 Crores)
- **Create E-Way Bills** for goods movement exceeding ₹50,000
- **Maintain accurate ledgers** (Cash, ITC, Liability)
- **Track return filing status** and deadlines

Non-compliance can result in interest, penalties, and operational disruptions.

### Compliance APIs

Our Compliance APIs cover the complete spectrum of GST operations:

- Verify GST-registered businesses and track return filing status without authentication.
- Complete GST portal integration for authenticated taxpayers from fetching business sales/purchase information, return filing, ledgers and invoice management system (IMS) operations.
- Generate, manage, and track E-Invoices seamlessly.
- Manage E-Way Bills for goods movement across India.

### Analytics APIs

Transform your GST data into actionable insights:

- GSTR-2A Reconciliation - Automatically reconcile your purchase data with supplier GSTR-1 filings to identify missing invoices, mismatches, and ITC eligibility.
- GSTR-2B Reconciliation - Reconcile with auto-drafted purchase data to ensure accurate ITC claims and compliance.
